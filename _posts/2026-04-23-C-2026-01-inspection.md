Installed the new nuc. Carniolans from Northwest Bee Supply. 5 deep frames, nuc was chock-a-block with bees. 3 full frames of capped brood, larvae, and eggs. Seemed to be out of stores.

I didn't see the queen, but the eggs implied she was there somewhere.

Installed a medium super with 5 or 6 frames of honey, fed 1:1, and added norroa.

It's really good to have bees back in this hive. I see it several times each day and activity of the bees makes me feel happier somehow. I was really sorry to have lost the last colony (C-2025-01) they were quite vigourous and entertaining. I'd sit our near their hive watching them often. Being the first, of hopefully many swarm catches, they we quite special.
