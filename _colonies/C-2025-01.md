A swarm that moved into stacked equipment (a deep and three mediums on a pallet) sometime around 2 May 2025 while the boxes were waiting to be cleaned out from the 2024 deadouts. Discovered and restacked on a level bottom board on 4 May 2025; lived in the driveway for its whole life.

Naturally requeened in late June 2025: a charged supersedure cell was observed on 15 June, then on 23 June the colony swarmed (bivouacked in the cedar trees) while I was repositioning the stand and ant-proofing it. The remaining colony raised a new queen and recovered well — by 2 August the new queen was laying, the colony had bounced back, and there were nine frames of honey plus pollen.

Lost over the winter of 2025–26; equipment in the driveway was reused on 23 April 2026 to install C-2026-01. End date is the date by which the loss was confirmed; actual death likely earlier in the winter.
